# Current experience: observations before the redesign

Captured in this task on 2026-11-11, using the public CookDance site in the in-app browser. Screenshots are stored in this folder.

1. **Homepage — functional, but presentation-led.** `01-current-home.png` shows a large brand introduction occupying the first viewport. The first content link is an invitation to explore; actual article subjects are not visible. Preserve the olive/near-white palette and restrained typography; move content into the first view.
2. **Learn — functional, but articles start below the fold.** `02-current-collection.png` shows a large heading and introduction filling the viewport before the two article cards. Reduce the introductory hierarchy and let a returning reader discover actual content immediately.
3. **Pinterest reference — partial public access.** `03-pinterest-reference.png` and `03-pinterest-public-reference.png` record a public header and partially visible imagery obstructed by a login prompt. No account was accessed. This is limited visual grounding, not a complete Pinterest usability audit. The user's own direction and the generated concepts determine the new CookDance flow.

The subsequent user direction superseded the initial editorial homepage proposal: only logo, slogan and photographs on the homepage; text and important supporting imagery after clicking. The third generated image is the revision of that selected Pinterest-like direction.

Screenshot observations do not establish full keyboard or screen-reader compliance. The prototype adds accessible names, descriptive image alternatives, visible keyboard focus, a skip link and native dialog semantics, but no full accessibility certification is claimed.
