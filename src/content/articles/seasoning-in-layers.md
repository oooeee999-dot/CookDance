Seasoning is a conversation with the dish. Instead of saving every adjustment for the end, taste at sensible points and consider what is missing.

## Build a baseline

Start with a modest amount of salt. As ingredients soften and liquids reduce, their flavor changes. Taste again before adding more.

## Think beyond salt

Acidity can make a rich dish feel brighter. Fat can soften sharp edges. Fresh herbs add a different character from herbs cooked for a long time. Make one small adjustment at a time so you can notice its effect.

## Finish by tasting

Taste the completed dish in the form you will serve it. A sauce alone may seem strong but balance well with pasta, grains, or vegetables.
