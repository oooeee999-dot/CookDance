An everyday knife should feel predictable in your hand. The best choice for one cook may feel awkward to another, so begin with comfort and control.

## Hold it before you decide

Notice the handle shape, weight, and balance. Can you grip it securely without strain? Can you guide the tip and heel through a comfortable chopping motion?

## Prioritize maintenance

A practical knife is one you can keep sharp and clean. A stable cutting board and safe storage matter as much as the blade itself.

## Buy for your cooking

Think about the ingredients you prepare most often and the space you have. One versatile knife is a sensible starting point for many home kitchens; you can add specialized tools when you know you need them.
