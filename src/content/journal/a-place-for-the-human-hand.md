We have been thinking about two directions at CookDance: the work we can do with AI, and the work for which we deliberately choose a slower, more personal process.

Both begin with the same question: what would serve this idea, and the person behind it, best?

Sometimes the answer is a new tool. Sometimes it is a pencil, a brush, a sheet of paper, and someone willing to spend time with them.

## The value of a mark

A hand-drawn line records a small decision. How firmly to press. Where to pause. Whether to leave a shape unfinished or try it again.

We find something moving in those decisions. A drawing can hold an artist’s way of looking, a memory, a conversation, or a detail that mattered to the person who commissioned it. Its value can come from knowing how it was made and whose attention it carries.

AI can produce images that resemble pencil marks or painted textures. That appearance does not make a generated image a drawing someone made by hand. For a project where the making matters, the actual process deserves a place in the brief.

We want to keep that place open.

## Hand-drawn illustration, made around your idea

CookDance now welcomes enquiries for hand-drawn illustration projects.

For a gift with a personal story behind it, explore our [wedding and anniversary keepsake illustrations](/journal/personalized-wedding-keepsake-illustrations/).

You might have a story to tell, a product that needs its own visual character, an editorial page, a piece of packaging, or a personal gift with a particular memory behind it. We would love to hear what you want the image to express.

Bring references if you have them. Tell us which details matter and where the illustration will live. A quiet pencil drawing, a loose ink line, and a colorful painted scene each ask for a different approach. We can work through the style, materials, composition, and delivery format together.

The goal is to make something considered for you, with room for the illustrator’s judgement and your own point of view.

## Care is part of the delivery

A beautiful image is only part of a good collaboration. Listening carefully, asking useful questions, and responding to feedback are part of the work too.

Before a project begins, we agree what will be made: the technique, the number of illustrations, the review stages, the timeline, and the intended uses. We also clarify whether you need digital files, a physical original, or both, along with any shipping and usage arrangements.

You should know what to expect, how the work will develop, and what you will receive.

## Cook for You

For us, **Cook for You** means paying attention to what your idea needs.

We remain curious about AI and what it makes possible. We also want to make room for the person holding the brush: their experience, their choices, and the care they bring to a page.

If your next project calls for a human hand, tell us about it. We would be glad to explore it with you.
