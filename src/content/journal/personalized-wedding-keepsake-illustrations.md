The place you first met. A journey you still talk about. The little dog who became part of the family. The home you made together.

A wedding or anniversary keepsake can bring those memories into one illustration: a small, personal world made around your story.

CookDance welcomes custom wedding, anniversary, and couple illustration projects, including hand-drawn portraits and illustrated story maps. Whether you are giving a gift to your partner, celebrating friends, or remembering your own wedding, we would love to hear what would make the piece meaningful to you.

## A picture of the things that matter

An illustrated love story map can connect several moments on a single page. A couple portrait can focus on one expression, one place, or one particularly happy day.

Your illustration might include:

- A couple portrait inspired by your photographs.
- The café, bookshop, street, or city where your story began.
- Favorite journeys, an engagement, a first home, or your wedding venue.
- Pets, flowers, shared hobbies, and other details with a place in your lives.
- Names, a date, or a short line that feels like you.

You do not need to arrive with a finished design. A few memories and a feeling for the style are enough to start a conversation.

## Tell us your story

Start with clear photographs and a short list of the moments you would like to include. Tell us who the gift is for, how you hope to use it, and when you need it.

References can help describe the mood: soft watercolor, loose pencil, delicate ink, or a colorful storybook feeling. We develop a new composition around your own memories, with the people, places, and details discussed with you.

Together, we agree the portrait references, scene selection, wording, color direction, size, and review stages before work begins. Names and dates receive a dedicated check before the final artwork is prepared.

## Agreeing the finished piece

If an original drawn by hand is important to you, include that in your brief. We will agree the drawing technique and materials, along with how the artwork will be reviewed and delivered.

Digital files, a physical original, printed copies, framing, and shipping are separate delivery choices to confirm. We discuss what is included, the budget, and the production schedule before you commit to the project. A frame in a concept image does not mean framing is included in every commission.

The example on this page is an AI-generated concept with a fictional couple and imagined milestones. It helps show one way a story could be arranged. A commissioned piece begins with your own story and an agreed making process.
